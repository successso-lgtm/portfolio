CASE 01 · EY HANYOUNG / LG.COM GLOBAL SEARCH

Diagnosing search friction across markets

Search performance is not just a metric problem: a decline can come from ranking logic, changes in traffic, customer intent or even automated behavior. My role was to distinguish these explanations before recommending a change.

Aug 2026 — Present
Search analyticsHypothesis testingClaude + ChatGPT reportingProduct requirements
The question I asked

When results look irrelevant, is the issue the query, the ranking engine, the product catalog—or the traffic itself?

01 / Diagnose

Isolate the mechanism

Compared query intent with actual result pages for model, size and color searches. Examined how keyword matching, promotional boosts and catalog availability affected top-ranked products.

02 / Test

Evaluate the trade-off

Supported UK/Spain ranking experiments, checking whether improvements in result relevance were also reflected in CTR instead of assuming that better matching always means better engagement.

03 / Operationalize

Make findings reusable

Recognized that monthly multi-country reporting repeatedly required processing new raw data and rebuilding presentation materials. Using Claude and ChatGPT, I developed a reusable Excel dashboard and reporting workflow so that loading fresh raw data updates the analytical outputs and report-ready materials.

CASE 02 · KAKAOPAY / INSURANCE CLAN — INSURANCE HUB PARTY

Improving conversion by understanding customer context

Rather than increasing campaign volume, I focused on why the same message might produce different results across customer groups—and how the operating model could support continuous improvement.

Sep 2025 — Apr 2026
SQL + BICRM experimentsRPA requirements + collaborationKPI management
The question I asked

What if low campaign response is not a copy problem, but a mismatch between the message, customer situation and moment of delivery?

01 / Segment

Look for meaningful context

Analyzed acquisition and engagement data using SQL, Grafana, Power BI and Excel. Reframed audiences using user lifecycle and insurance-ownership signals rather than treating all recipients as one population.

02 / Experiment

Change variables deliberately

Refined targeting, message wording, offers and channel/timing choices. Monitored response and conversion behavior to decide which combinations warranted further iteration.

03 / Scale operations

Remove repetitive work

Each day, a SQL query was used to extract eligible audiences for outbound messaging. I identified this repeated extraction as an RPA opportunity, documented the current steps and functional requirements, and collaborated with the RPA team so the process could be handled through an RPA bot.

Why automation belonged in the solution

Daily audience extraction with SQL was a repeated operational step before campaign messaging. Rather than claiming to build the bot myself, I translated the business process into an RPA requirements document and worked with the specialist RPA team on automating that step.

How stakeholders were aligned

Used recurring KPI reviews to connect marketing priorities with operational constraints and decide what to test or change next.

Iterative targeting and messaging work contributed to campaign performance improvement. Separately, I supported RPA-based audience extraction through workflow mapping and functional requirements in partnership with the RPA team; no unsupported time-savings figure is claimed.

CASE 03 · ABLY / GENERATIVE AI OPERATIONS

Turning impressive AI demos into repeatable workflows

Testing video generation is easy; deciding whether it is dependable enough for commerce operations requires a different framework. I examined model outputs through content quality, consistency and operating suitability.

Jul — Aug 2026
HiggsfieldSeedanceModel evaluationAI operationalization
The question I asked

How do we turn a generative video model from a promising test into a repeatable content-production process?

01 / Explore

Test fit, not just novelty

Applied and compared Higgsfield and Seedance-based generative video approaches to commerce-content use cases, examining visual output and suitability for real workflows.

02 / Define quality

Make judgments consistent

Worked with mood classification and Rookie Pass/Fail criteria to structure what constituted a usable output, rather than rely only on subjective impressions.

03 / Iterate

Feed results back to the process

Reviewed recurring model-output issues and used evaluation feedback to refine repeatable assessment and production workflows.

CASE 04 · NAVER AI RUSH / HYPERCLOVA X

Choosing a Korean-specialized model for a language-sensitive problem

A healthcare assistant depends on how well it interprets what users actually say. Korean symptom descriptions can be colloquial and context-dependent, so model choice was part of the service strategy—not merely a technology label.

2023
HyperCLOVA XKorean language AIPrompt designPrototype
The question I asked

Could a Korean-language-specialized LLM make symptom intake more natural and help guide users toward an appropriate next step?

01 / Select

Fit model to the user

Selected HyperCLOVA X for its Korean-language focus, considering the nuances of how people describe discomfort and symptoms.

02 / Structure

Give responses context

Organized 200+ healthcare reference items and designed prompt and conversation flows that linked symptom context to relevant healthcare options.

03 / Prototype

Make the idea testable

Worked with developers to produce a functional prototype and present the service logic; selected as a Best Practice project.
